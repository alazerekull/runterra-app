How It Works

1

Start a Run

Open RunTerra, hit Start, and begin running. GPS automatically tracks your route.

2

Claim Tiles

Every hexagon you pass through gets claimed in your color. The more you run, the more you own.

3

Dominate the Leaderboard

Compete solo or join a clan. Accumulate tiles and climb to the top of the global rankings.
